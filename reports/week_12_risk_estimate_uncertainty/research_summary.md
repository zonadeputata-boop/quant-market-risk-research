# Research summary — Week 12

Across 69 month-end estimates, extending the history from 63 to 252 sessions reduced the median relative width of the volatility interval from 37.9% to 30.0%. For 97.5% historical ES, the corresponding change was only 45.0% to 44.4%. The empirical message is therefore narrower than “more data solves uncertainty”: it helped the volatility estimate in this sample, while tail-risk uncertainty remained wide.

At the latest endpoint (14 August 2026), the 252-day estimates were 9.16% annualized volatility (95% bootstrap interval 7.78%-10.51%) and 1.57% daily ES (1.15%-1.91%). Block-length sensitivity is supplied in the tables.

All public numbers are generated by the packaged script and checked by automated tests. See `methodology.md` for scope and limitations.
