# Research summary

The post explains the interaction between funding liquidity and market liquidity. The theoretical mechanism follows Brunnermeier and Pedersen (2009). The current policy context follows the FSB's December 2024 recommendations and the BIS March 2026 executive summary. The 2022 UK gilt episode provides an official empirical case study from the Bank of England.

The numerical carousel example is a deterministic balance-sheet scenario. It starts from $250 of assets, $150 of debt and $100 of equity, applies a 4% asset shock, then restores a 2.5x leverage limit by selling assets and repaying debt. After each sale, a specified linear price-impact sensitivity applies to the remaining position. The model iterates until leverage returns to the limit.

The scenario isolates the feedback mechanism and keeps every assumption visible. It does not estimate a causal market-impact function or probability. The empirical figures in the case study support the mechanism's relevance but do not calibrate the model.
