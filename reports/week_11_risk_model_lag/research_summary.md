# Week 11 research summary

## Answer first

Covariance memory materially changed how quickly the fixed portfolio risk estimate reacted. Across 12 separated 99th-percentile absolute-return events, the median forecast revision was 25.8% for EWMA(0.94) and 20.0% for rolling 21D, compared with 6.4% for rolling 63D and 4.9% for rolling 126D.

The faster estimates also moved more in ordinary daily operation. Mean absolute daily revisions were 3.21%-3.63% for EWMA and 21D, versus 0.64%-1.22% for 126D and 63D.

In the 1,516-date forward evaluation, EWMA had the lowest MAE at 2.34 annualized volatility points. The difference versus 21D (2.39) and 63D (2.43) was modest and no estimator won every calendar year. The evidence supports a speed-stability tradeoff, not a universal winner.
