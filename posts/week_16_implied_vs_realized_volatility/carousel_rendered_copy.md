## 2. Two measures, two time directions

Two measures, two time directions

The comparison works only when the underlying, horizon and annualization are aligned.

IMPLIED VOLATILITY

Observed today

Inferred from option prices.
Forward-looking and risk-neutral.

Known before the period starts.

REALIZED VOLATILITY

Measured afterward

Calculated from returns over
the completed future window.

Known only after the period ends.

Today’s VIX should be compared with the next 30-day realized volatility, not trailing volatility.

## 7. When realized volatility wins

When realized volatility wins

Implied volatility is known up front. Realized volatility can finish above it.

Dimension / Ordinary regime / Stress regime
Observed P&L / Premium accrual can dominate / Gap losses can dominate
Distribution / Many small gains / Negative skew and tail loss
Hedging / Liquidity usually available / Slippage and discontinuous moves
Funding / Margin manageable / Margin calls and forced deleveraging

A positive average gap does not cap the loss in the wrong path.
