# Methodology — Week 12

## Question

How much sampling uncertainty surrounds common volatility and historical Expected Shortfall estimates?

## Data and portfolio

Adjusted closes for SPY, QQQ, TLT, GLD, HYG and SHY, 2 January 2020 through 14 August 2026. The diagnostic portfolio is rebalanced conceptually to fixed equal weights for daily return aggregation. Returns are simple close-to-close returns. Prices were downloaded from the Yahoo Finance chart endpoint on 15 August 2026 UTC and are packaged with the analysis.

## Estimators

- Annualized volatility: sample standard deviation of portfolio returns times sqrt(252).
- One-day 97.5% historical ES: mean of the worst `ceil((1 - 0.975) * n)` positive losses. This makes the finite-sample tail rule explicit: 2 observations for n=63 and 7 for n=252.
- Evaluation timestamps: final trading session of each month after at least 252 return observations are available; 69 month-ends.

## Uncertainty procedure

For each endpoint and window, draw 2,000 circular moving-block bootstrap samples of the same length, using blocks of 10 consecutive observations with wrap-around. Re-estimate both statistics and use the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles as a 95% bootstrap percentile interval. The latest endpoint is also recomputed with block lengths 5, 10 and 21.

The block bootstrap is used because return observations may exhibit short-range dependence, including volatility clustering. The block length is a modeling choice, not a fact.

## Interpretation and limitations

These are conditional resampling intervals, not guarantees of unconditional coverage. They describe variation generated by reusing patterns present in the chosen historical window. They do not account for unseen regimes, structural breaks, changing exposures, stale or erroneous prices, model misspecification, or tail outcomes absent from the sample. Month-end estimates use overlapping histories and therefore are not independent observations. Results are descriptive and make no performance or investment claim.
